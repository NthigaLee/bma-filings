📋 About Bermuda FCRs & ECR Ratios

The Enhanced Capital Requirement (ECR) ratio measures a company's eligible capital relative to the capital requirement determined by the BMA's BSCR (Bermuda Solvency Capital Requirement) model. A ratio above 100% means the company holds more capital than required. The market average was approximately 224% in 2024 (down from 263% in 2023). FCRs are published annually by each insurer and cover business performance, governance, risk profile, solvency valuation, and capital management.
